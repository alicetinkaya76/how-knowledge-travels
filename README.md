# How Knowledge Travels: Islam in Different Cultures

Exploring the history of science through translation, learning and the development of new knowledge.

Dr. Ali Çetinkaya’s English-language presentation for *Feel the Islamic Spirit*, 22 November 2026.

[Open the 90-minute presentation](https://alicetinkaya76.github.io/how-knowledge-travels/). The 36 slides follow Arabic and Latin scientific texts, the people who taught from them, the objects they carried, and the words that travelled with them. The final five minutes are for audience questions.

Use the arrow keys to open reveals and advance. Press **F** for full screen, **B** to hide or restore the controls, **M** for contents and **C** for sources and image credits. Select archival images to enlarge them. Leaflet maps support zoom, pan and place markers. The paper map animates five historical legs, and the maps have an optional online street layer. The single HTML file embeds the archival images, fonts, demonstrations and Natural Earth map for offline use.
